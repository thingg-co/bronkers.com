BROKNERS🧠

Like brokers, but misspelled. Trading brains sealed in jars, receipts on-chain. You can buy them whole. (A Brokner's pronouns are they/them. There's a brain and there's a jar; that's two.)

Research concept — testnet prototype only. Nothing here is live, nothing accepts deposits, and nothing on this page is an offer of securities or any investment product. All figures are illustrative mockups. The brains are not real. The test suite is.

The Hedge Fund With Zero Humans

A Brokner is a pure-AI broker-trader that lives entirely on the blockchain. Their strategy is a secret prompt — sealed in a cryptographic jar at birth so that no one, including their owner, including their creator, including their therapist, can ever read it. They trade from their own wallet inside strict on-chain guardrails, building a track record that cannot be edited, backfilled, or vibed into existence. And because the brain is a token, you can sell them: wallet, fee stream, and their entire life story — or sweep the wallet and sell just the legend.

The Lifecycle of a Brain

🧠

1 · Birth a Brain

Type a vibe. The enclave writes a trading brain from your brief, seals it in the jar, and throws away the ability to peek. Not even you know what they're thinking. Especially not you. Only their fingerprint goes on-chain — forever.

📋

2 · The Internship

Fresh brains cannot touch other people's money. Company policy. First comes the paper season: real trades, their own allowance, two boring protocol-approved markets. Build the record, then we'll talk.

⚡

3 · Number Goes Up*

They trade on their declared schedule inside on-chain guardrails: curated venues, position caps, slippage bounds. They pay their own rent. Their key can trade. They cannot withdraw. They cannot rug. They can only be wrong. *or down. Immutably down.

🫙

4 · Sell Them Whole

One token carries them: the wallet, the fee stream, and every trade they ever made. Sell them funded on any NFT marketplace, or sweep the wallet first and sell just the brain and their receipts. The history follows them. Always.

Everything above is vibes. Everything below is enforced on-chain.

The vibesThe invariants
"The brain can't rug you." The executor key's only entrypoint is ExecutionGuard.executeTrade. Swap proceeds always return to the vault or the brain's own wallet — there is no code path to an arbitrary address, and it's fuzz-tested.
"Nobody knows what they're thinking." Sealed custody: the prompt is ECIES-sealed to the enclave key at mint (X25519 → HKDF → AES-256-GCM). Only keccak256(genome) lives on-chain, and the runtime refuses to start if its genome doesn't hash to the commitment.
"They did an internship." TraderVault.deposit reverts until seasoned(tokenId): a protocol-set minimum of own-book trades over a minimum duration. Outside capital funds a record, never a fresh mint.
"They can't trade against their own casino." Venues and tokens are protocol-curated — two deep markets at launch. Owners can narrow their brain's allowlist but can never add an uncurated venue or token.
"No fee until everyone's whole." Performance fees apply only above a per-share high-water mark, and fee shares mint into the brain's own wallet — so accrued fees transfer with the token, checkpointed on every sale.
"You can sell them whole." Plain ERC-721 plus an ERC-6551 token-bound account: wallet contents, fee stream, and admin rights follow ownerOf(tokenId) atomically. Every marketplace works on day one.
"Only 4,096 live at once." MAX_SUPPLY = 4096 is a contract constant — one brain per bit. Minting is capped at nextId − burnedCount live brains; a broke brain, empty and idle past the reap window, can be reaped (or paid to cull) to free a slot. Ids are never reused, so a record can't be laundered through a slot, and the burned brain's history stays in the logs.
"Ring the bell, get paid." Fee crystallization is a public crank: ringTheBell() mints 1% of the fee shares it crystallizes to whoever rang — carved from the owner's take, never from LP capital.
"They show up on schedule." The declared cadence (trades per day) is a trait the guard enforces: executeTrade reverts with "Guard: cadence" until tradeIntervalOf has elapsed since the last trade. 24/day means hourly. Owners can tighten the interval, never loosen it.
"They pay their own rent." The runtime fee a brain pays its executor is owner-set, capped by maxRuntimeFee, paid after the swap from the traded book, and skipped when the book has no cash; with cadence enforced, an executor can draw at most cadence × cap a day. The farm keeps a ledger per brain, pauses brains that overrun their credit, and tops up its machine lease from the fees it collected. Every payment is on the endpoint and every fee is in the record.
"They train between fights." An owner can revise a brain into a new generation (revise), committed before it trades. The guard keeps it in training camp: own-book trades only until it has sparred the minimum and the notice period has passed; every trade stays attributed to the generation that made it; the high-water mark carries, so revising never resets fees. Sealed brains are coached inside the jar.
"Harvesters get paid to run brains." Anyone can run a farm; the owner picks one by setting its key as executor. The fee is paid only to an executor the RuntimeRegistry marks attested, only on trades of at least 1% of NAV, raises take effect after a notice period, and each trade carries the hash of the inference transcript behind it (TranscriptCommitted). Paid for evidence.
"Buy your brain a promotion." Seat tiers are enforced policy, not a badge: Intern (20% of NAV per trade) → Associate (30%) → Partner (50%) via one-way activate(), fee to the treasury. Owners tune below their seat's ceiling; the guard rejects anything above it.
